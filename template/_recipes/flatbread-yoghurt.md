## Ingredients

|Quantity|Ingredient
|300 grams|plain yoghurt
|280 grams|self-raising flour (and more for rolling)
|4  grams|salt
|butter|for brushing


## Method

1. In a large bowl, mix the following
    - 300 grams plain yoghurt
    - 4 grams salt
    - 280 grams self-raising flour
   The mixture will be quite wet and cold, don't let it rest or warm up.
2. Divide the mixture into 6 equal portions
3. Heat a pan on medium high
4. Generously flour a rolling surface and roll out a dough portion until it is very thin, less than 4 mm thick.
5. Cook on one side for 1 minute or until bubbles begin to form, before cooking on the other side for 1 minute.
6. Remove from pan and butter one side
7. Repeat with remaining dough.
