## Ingredients

|Quantity|Ingredient
|---|---
|1 cup|plain flour
|1 cup|polenta
|1 tsp (4 grams)|salt
|4 tsp (16 grams)| baking powder
|1/3 cup|oil (vegetable/canola/neutral tasting oil)
|1 cup|milk
|1|egg

## Method

1. Heat oven to 200 degrees celcius
2. In a large bowl, mix the following dry ingredients
    - 1 cup plaing flour
    - 1 cup polenta
    - 1 tsp or 4 grams salt
    - 4 tsp or 16 grams baking powder
3. Add the wet ingredients to the bowl, and combine
    - 1/3 cup oil
    - 1 cup milk
    - 1 egg
4. Add the mixture to a greased and lined loaf tin
5. Cook for 28 minutes
