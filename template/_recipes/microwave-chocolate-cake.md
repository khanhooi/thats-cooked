## Ingredients

|Quantity|Ingredient
|---|---
|175g|caster sugar
|140g|plain flour
|30g| cocoa powder
|12g|baking powder
|100 ml or 83g|vegetable oil
|2|large eggs
|1 tsp|vanilla essence
|50ml|hot water


## Method

1. Grease a microwavable silicone loaf pan with butter or oil
2. In a large bowl, mix the following dry ingredients
    - 175g caster sugar
    - 140g plain flour
    - 30g cocoa powder
    - 12g baking powder
3. In another bowl, mix the following wet ingredients
    - 100ml vegetable oil
    - 2 large eggs
    - 1 tsp vanilla essense
4. Mix the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
5. Add 50 ml of hot water and mix it in
6. pour mixture into loaf pan
7. On a 800w microwave, it takes 5 minutes at full power. Skewer should come out clean in the middle.
